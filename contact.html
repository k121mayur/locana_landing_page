Knowledgebase & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning field studies, ethical protocols, and network membership.

How quickly can Locana deploy a certified field fleet?

Because our network of certified field professionals is permanently mapped across 700+ districts in 28 states, we do not hire from scratch per project. Standard deployment readiness is 48 hours following protocol freeze and questionnaire digitization.

How are field professionals compensated on Locana assignments?

Field professionals receive transparent day-rates based on role complexity and verified district assignments, with guaranteed prompt disbursement directly to their bank accounts following quality-audited completion.

What quality audits are built into every institutional study?

Every engagement includes: (1) Real-time GPS polygon and timestamp telemetry, (2) Audio consent snippet validation, (3) Minimum 10% independent supervisor back-checks, and (4) AI-powered anomaly detection on response velocity and clustering.

What languages and dialects are covered for Vernacular AI?

We support all 22 Eighth Schedule Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam, Punjabi, Assamese, Maithili, Santali, Kashmiri, Nepali, Konkani, Dogri, Sindhi, Bodo, Sanskrit, Manipuri) plus over 150 regional spoken dialects.

How do you protect respondent data privacy and FPIC consent?

We adhere strictly to Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC) protocols delivered in the respondent's primary dialect. All PII data is segregated, encrypted, and processed in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) standards.
