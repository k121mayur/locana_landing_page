The Structural Divide

THE LAST-MILE CHALLENGE

The last mile is not one problem. It is a combination of distance, access, information, skills and trust.

Digital

Connectivity gaps, low digital familiarity and device friction can exclude remote communities.

Physical

Distributed settlements, difficult terrain and fragmented local logistics make execution harder.

Information

Local context, language and ground realities are often missing from conventional datasets.

Skills

Institutions need trained frontline people who can execute standardised work locally.

Trust

Local relationships and culturally appropriate engagement matter when working with communities.

Operating Workflow

HOW LOCANA WORKS

A universal 6-step lifecycle transforming complex institutional requirements into verified ground outcomes.

STEP 01

Scope

Understand the objective, geography, scale, protocol and timeline.

STEP 02

Match

Identify the right local partners based on location, language, skills and requirement.

STEP 03

Mobilise

Brief, train, assign tools and deploy the field team.

STEP 04

Execute

Local partners perform the agreed task using standardised workflows.

STEP 05

Verify

Quality checks, supervisor review, validation and exception handling.

STEP 06

Deliver

Provide the agreed data, service, outcome or local execution report.

1. Scoping & Protocols

Institution shares target geography, sample/task requirements, protocol and timeline.

2. Partner Mapping

Locana maps the requirement to suitable certified local partners.

3. Digital Tool Deployment

Digital forms, training and field instructions are deployed.

4. Field Execution

Local partners execute the task with appropriate consent and location controls.

5. Verification & QC

Quality checks and supervisor back-checks are completed as applicable.

6. Delivery & Settlement

Validated outputs are delivered and local partners are paid according to the agreed process.
