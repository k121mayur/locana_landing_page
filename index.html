The 6-Pillar Quality Architecture

Quality isn't an afterthought QC pass — it is engineered into the protocol design, field certification, and real-time telemetry validation.

01 / TEAM INTEGRITY

Ethically Certified Local Teams

Every interviewer completes mandatory certification in research ethics, FPIC consent, POSH, and sensitive community engagement prior to deployment.

02 / PROTOCOL RIGOR

Rigorous Field Readiness

In-depth mock interviews, vernacular translation sanity checks, dry runs, and pilot debriefs ensure flawless execution across remote locations.

03 / REAL-TIME DETECTION

AI-Enabled Error Detection

CAPI workflows equipped with real-time logic gates, outlier triggers, velocity heuristics, and automatic duplicate response suppression.

04 / TRANSPARENCY

Live Institutional Dashboards

Clients receive live telemetry feeds tracking real-time survey rates, district quotas, enumerator performance, and spatial clustering.

05 / AUDIT TRAIL

GPS & Multimedia Validation

Every record carries cryptographically hashed GPS polygon coordinates, device timestamps, ambient audio snippets, and photo metadata.

06 / DEFENSIVE AUDIT

Multi-Layer Supervisor Checks

Minimum 10% independent back-checks, unannounced supervisor spot checks, and telephonic re-interviews built into contract deliverables.
