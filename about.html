The Locana Philosophy

Because dashboards only imply.
Ground truth confirms.

In an era of satellite extrapolation and generative AI models, the gap between top-down data and ground-level reality has never been wider. Locana exists to bridge that divide with human presence, cultural fluency, and auditable verification.

Our Vision

Trusted ground truth, everywhere.

To build India's most trusted ethical, hyperlocal field intelligence network — ensuring last-mile ground truth is verified, auditable, and valued.

Our Mission

How we deliver.

  • — Certify local professionals across 225+ active districts and 25+ states.
  • — Enforce consent-first rigor with audio consent verification and location audit logs.
  • — Empower researchers with fair day-rates and career certification tiers.
Ethical Foundation

Data dignity is non-negotiable.

We operate on a strict ethical charter to ensure mutual value creation for communities and researchers:

Free, Prior & Informed Consent (FPIC)

Every participant is briefed in native dialect, with audio or written consent recorded for full auditability.

Gender-Matched Fieldwork

Interviewer gender matching ensures respondent psychological safety and candid response accuracy.

Fair Pay & Professional Growth

Structured day-rate bands (₹500–₹2,000/day + rev-share) provide dignified rural employment.
